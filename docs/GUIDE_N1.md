# Night 1 guide

A step-by-step walkthrough for surviving Night 1 of PROJECT NULL. Night 1 is forgiving: a player who follows this guide survives about **99% of the time** (measured over 300 simulated nights with a scripted player that plays exactly like this).

The night lasts **11 real minutes**. Each in-game hour takes about 1 minute 50 seconds.

## The short version

If you remember nothing else, remember these five rules:

1. **Scraping in the vents? Fan off, radio off, hands off the doors.** The Echo is the creature that ends most first nights. It can only drop in while the station is loud.
2. **Something at a door? Shut that door.** Open it again when the doorway camera is empty.
3. **The tall one in a corridor? Light that corridor.** Light keeps the Wanderer away.
4. **Glance at the pale one every 20 seconds or so. Never stare.** A two-second look is enough.
5. **Heavy breathing at the West door after 3 AM? Pull the field lever and hold it about 22 seconds.** That sends the Patient home.

## Before you start

- **Play the Practice Night first** if you have not yet. Nothing can get you there, a live map shows every creature, and a coach walks you through each of the rules above.
- **Leave the radio briefing on** (Settings → Display and comfort). R. explains each system right as it becomes relevant.
- **No-startle mode** (first screen or Settings) softens every sound and light change. Night 1 has no jumpscares either way.

## Controls you will use

| Action | Key |
| --- | --- |
| Sit at the workstation / use a panel | `E` |
| Switch camera | `1`–`9`, `0`, `-`, `=`, or click the map |
| Repair, reset or brace | Hold `E` |
| Pause | `Esc` |

The workstation does **not** pause the night. Neither does reading a document.

## Know your station

You sit in the security station. Things reach you in only three ways:

| Way in | Who uses it | Your defence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **West door** (CAM 10) | Wanderer, Observer, Patient | Top button on the door panel shuts it. |
| **East door** (CAM 11) | Wanderer, Observer | Same. |
| **Duct above you** (no camera) | Echo only | Silence. The vent grille shakes and sheds dust while the Echo is inside it. |

Each door panel has two buttons: **top** shuts the door, **bottom** lights the corridor behind it (West Corridor CAM 08, East Corridor CAM 09). The **field lever** is on the back wall. The **fan switch** and the **radio** are in the station too.

The two desk screens either side of the camera monitor matter:

- **Left: facility terminal.** Messages, gate releases and the LOG tab.
- **Right: acoustic monitor.** A yellow line shows how loud the station is. The dashed red line is the Echo's threshold. **Yellow under red means the Echo cannot get in.**

The cameras worth knowing:

| Camera | Room | Why you look |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CAM 01 | Containment A | The Wanderer's cell. |
| CAM 02 | Containment B | The Observer's cell. |
| CAM 03 | Isolation Ward | The Patient's cell. |
| CAM 04 | Acoustics Lab | The Echo's lab. |
| CAM 05 / 06 / 07 | Research Hall, Cold Storage, Central Junction | Where things pass through on their way to you. |
| CAM 08 / 09 | West / East Corridor | The last rooms before the doorways. |
| CAM 10 / 11 | West / East Door | Something here is right outside. |
| CAM 12 | Ventilation Shaft | The Echo's route to the duct. |

## Step by step

### 12:00 to 12:30: settle in

Nothing is loose yet. Every cell is locked.

1. Sit at the workstation (`E` on the middle desk screen).
2. Click through all twelve cameras once so you know what each room looks like empty.
3. Look at the acoustic monitor. With the fan and radio on, the yellow line sits **above** the red line. That is fine for now; the Echo is still sealed in.
4. Listen to R. on the radio. Every message is also in the terminal's LOG tab.

### 12:30: Containment A opens (the Wanderer)

The tall one is out. It is slow to decide and easy to turn back.

1. Every 20 seconds or so, check CAM 07 (Central Junction) and the corridors, CAM 08 and CAM 09.
2. **When it is in a corridor or at the junction, light that corridor** (bottom button on that side's door panel). It refuses to enter a lit corridor most of the time, and if the light comes on while it stands there, it usually backs off.
3. **If it reaches a doorway (CAM 10 or 11), shut that door.** After two failed tries it retreats two rooms.
4. Turn the light off when it has gone. Each light costs power.

On Night 1 the Wanderer only reaches a door in about 4 of 10 nights. The light usually does the job.

### 12:54: the Acoustics Lab opens (the Echo)

This is the important one. **On Night 1, almost every loss is the Echo.** A player who never silences the station survives only about 1 night in 8.

1. Listen for **metal scraping in the ventilation**. A subtitle shows it too. You can also see it on CAM 12 (Ventilation Shaft).
2. **As soon as you hear it, switch the fan off and the radio off.** The yellow line on the acoustic monitor drops well below the red line.
3. When it reaches the duct right above you, you hear scraping overhead and **the vent grille trembles and sheds dust** (the "snow"). Keep everything off. You have several seconds after it arrives, and it cannot come down while the station is quiet.
4. While it is overhead, **avoid moving a door, repairing or resetting anything.** A moving door makes a short burst of noise that can push you over the line for a moment even when everything else is off. A door that is already shut is quiet enough.
5. **Never pull the field lever while the Echo is overhead.** The field is the loudest thing in the station.
6. When the scraping moves away and the grille is still, switch the fan back on.

The fan keeps the camera rack cool. With the fan off and the cameras in use, the rack overheats after about 80 seconds and the cameras shut down for a while. An Echo visit is usually much shorter than that, but turn the fan back on as soon as the Echo leaves.

The Echo first reaches the duct around **2:30** on a typical night, sometimes as early as 1:30, and comes back two or three times.

Seeing the Echo on a door camera is not a threat. It wanders the corridors sometimes, but the doors do not let it through. Only the duct does.

### 1:00: Containment B opens (the Observer)

The pale one moves only when nobody is watching it, but being stared at makes it restless.

1. **Every 20 seconds or so, find it on camera and look for two or three seconds.** That is enough for it to notice and freeze.
2. **Do not stare.** The longer you watch, the more agitated it gets. Agitation makes its rolls stronger, and a very agitated Observer stops freezing altogether.
3. **Do not forget it either.** After a minute without a look, it heads for you much more directly.
4. It usually comes through Cold Storage (CAM 06) or the Research Hall (CAM 05), then to the **West door** more often than the East.
5. **If it reaches a doorway, shut that door.** After three failed tries it retreats two rooms.

It reaches a door in about 8 of 10 nights, typically around 3:50, sometimes from 2:15.

### 2:00: the Isolation bulkhead opens (the Patient)

The bulkhead opens at 2:00, but on Night 1 the Patient **stays asleep until 3 AM**. You can ignore it for an hour.

### 3:00 to 6:00: the Patient

From 3 AM the big one starts moving. It is slow (one step every 8 seconds), and it comes almost exclusively to the **West door**, straight from the Isolation Ward through the West Corridor.

1. From 3 AM, include CAM 03 (Isolation Ward) and CAM 08 (West Corridor) in your checks. Through the camera speaker you can hear it breathing.
2. **When it reaches the West door (CAM 10), pull the field lever** on the back wall. While the field is on, it cannot come in at all, even through an open door.
3. **Hold the field for about 22 seconds.** Then it gives up and walks all the way back to Isolation. If you let go briefly, its progress is not lost; it fades slowly.
4. Do **not** open the West door while it stands there. It hears the door open and takes an extra try a few seconds later.
5. A shut door only slows it. After five pushes the door motor breaks: the door swings open and stays open until you repair it.

It reaches the West door in about 3 of 4 nights, typically around 4:45 and rarely before 4:00. It usually comes once.

**The one tricky moment: the Patient at the door and the Echo in the duct at the same time.** The field is too loud while the Echo listens. Do this instead:

1. If the West door is open, shut it now, before the Echo gets settled overhead. A shut door holds the Patient for a while; on Night 1 that is usually more than a minute.
2. Keep the fan and radio off and wait for the Echo to leave.
3. Then pull the field lever.

### 5:00 to 6:00: hold on

All four are at their strongest now, but on Night 1 that is still low. Keep the routine going. At 6 AM the night ends on its own.

## The routine

Once everything is loose, repeat this loop every 15 to 20 seconds:

1. **Listen.** Scraping in the vents means quiet now. Breathing or footsteps on the camera speaker tell you something is close.
2. **Doorways.** CAM 10 and CAM 11. Something there? Shut that door.
3. **Corridors.** CAM 08 and CAM 09. The Wanderer there? Light it.
4. **The Observer.** Find it and look for two or three seconds.
5. **After 3 AM: the Patient.** CAM 03, then CAM 08 and CAM 10.
6. **Power.** Open doors and turn off lights you no longer need.

## Power

You start with 100%. A careful player ends Night 1 with **about 25 to 30%** left, and rarely below 15%.

| What | Cost |
| --- | --- |
| A shut door | 6.6% per minute |
| A lit corridor | 3.3% per minute |
| The containment field | 9% per minute, so about 3.3% to send the Patient home |
| Everything else together (lights, cameras, fan, radio, base load) | about 3 to 4% per minute |

- Shut doors are the biggest drain. **Open a door as soon as its doorway camera is empty.**
- If power drops below about 12%, discharge the **auxiliary cell** in the electrical room (hold `E`) for +15%. It works once per night.
- At 0% the power fails, but you do not die automatically. The doors can be braced by hand at their yellow manual release (hold `E`), and the auxiliary cell can bring the power back. Creatures are a little more aggressive in the dark.

## When things go wrong

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A door says **MOTOR FAULT** | Something forced it. Go to the electrical room through the rear door and hold `E` on that door's breaker. Wait until the doorway is clear first, and never while the Echo is overhead. |
| A camera shows only static | A feed is down. Reset the signal relay panel (hold `E`, costs 0.5%). |
| All cameras went dark | The rack overheated because the fan was off too long. Switch the fan back on; the cameras come back after a short while. |
| Two things at once | Deal with the Echo first (silence costs nothing and it is the most dangerous), then doors, then the field. |
| Everything went dark | Power failure. Hold `E` on the manual releases of any door with something behind it, and use the auxiliary cell if you have not yet. |

## What ends a Night 1 run

From 300 simulated nights:

| Play style | Survival |
| --- | --- |
| Follows this guide | 99% |
| Follows this guide but never checks the Observer | 97% |
| Uses doors, lights and the field but never silences the station for the Echo | 12% |
| Only uses doors | 8% |
| Does nothing | 0% (usually caught around 2:00 to 2:30) |

The lesson: **on Night 1, the Echo is the creature to respect.** Everything else gives you plenty of time.

*These numbers come from the game's own simulation: 300 nights per play style, with the scripted player in `src/ai/ScriptedPlayers.js` reacting within 1.5 seconds. Real reactions are slower, so expect a little less in practice. Guides for Nights 2 to 5 will follow.*
