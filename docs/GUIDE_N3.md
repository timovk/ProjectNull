# Night 3 guide

Night 3 is the middle of the campaign and the first night where **power and noise start to fight each other**. The Patient comes early and often, the Echo is in the vents most of the night, and the two of them overlap almost twice a night.

This guide assumes the [Night 1 guide](GUIDE_N1.md) and the six habits from the [Night 2 guide](GUIDE_N2.md). Those habits are what decide Night 3.

## Step by step

### 12:00 to 12:54: set up and watch the back route

1. Station lights to **low** on the emergency power panel.
2. The Patient starts at level 3 and uses the back route through Cold Storage. Check **CAM 03** (Isolation Ward) and **CAM 06** (Cold Storage) every few checks.
3. From 12:30, the Wanderer. Light a corridor only while it is in it.

### 12:54 to 2:00: the Echo arrives early

The Echo usually reaches the vents within half an hour of its lab opening.

1. Scraping means **fan off, radio off**, and leave the doors as they are.
2. It leaves after about **30 seconds of unbroken silence**. Then fan back on.
3. Expect it back within a few minutes. It only backs off two rooms.

The Observer is out from 1:00. Keep the short glances going every 20 to 30 seconds.

### 2:00 to 5:00: the Patient routine

The Patient now comes to the West door three or four times a night (very occasionally to the East door).

- **Echo away:** field alone, door open, about 22 seconds. You will hear it withdraw (`[The weight behind the door shifts, and slowly withdraws]`).
- **Echo overhead:** shut the West door, stay silent, count the groans. Each groan is one push; the fifth breaks the motor. At these levels a shut door usually holds for more than a minute, and the Echo gives up after about half of that. The moment the vents go quiet, pull the field lever.
- **Door broken (MOTOR FAULT):** the field still works. Hold it until the Patient leaves, then repair the motor in the electrical room once the doorway and vents are clear.

### 5:00 to 6:00: the hard hour

About 7 in 10 Night 3 losses happen in the last hour. Arrive there with power to spare:

- If you are below about 25% at 4 AM, tighten up: lights off the moment they are not needed, doors open as soon as the doorway is clear.
- Use the auxiliary cell (+15%) when the vents are quiet, somewhere below 15%. Do not wait for the power to run out, and never discharge it with the Echo in the shaft or duct: the bang lets it in.

## Power

A careful player ends Night 3 with about **18%** left, and about 1 run in 10 drops under 10%. Most runs use the auxiliary cell. A full power failure is rare (about 2 in 100) if you follow the habits; without them it happens in about 1 run in 6.

## What ends Night 3 runs

| Play style | Survival |
| --- | --- |
| Night 1 guide only | 75% |
| With the six habits | 94% |
| With the six habits, but slower (5 s reactions, Observer glance every 40 s) | 92% |

With the habits, the Patient causes about half of the remaining losses, nearly always while the Echo was overhead and the field had to wait. The Echo causes most of the rest, usually right after a door moved.
