# Night 4 guide

On Night 4 there is rarely a quiet minute. The Patient can be at your door **before 1 AM**, the Echo visits about eight times, and all four subjects are often near the station at once. Good habits still carry you through most nights, but now one mistake with noise or power can end the run.

This guide assumes the [Night 1 guide](GUIDE_N1.md) and the six habits from the [Night 2 guide](GUIDE_N2.md).

## Step by step

### 12:00 to 1:00: the Patient comes first

1. Station lights to **low**.
2. **Watch the Patient from the start.** At level 4 it walks the back route through Cold Storage (CAM 06) to the West Corridor (CAM 08) straight away. Have the field ready.
3. The Wanderer leaves at 12:30, the Echo at 12:54. Both arrive within minutes.

### 1:00 to 5:00: keep the loop tight

Run the routine every 15 seconds or so. The order matters:

1. **Listen first.** Scraping means silence before anything else.
2. **Doorways** (CAM 10, CAM 11). Shut a door for the Wanderer or Observer. Field alone for the Patient, unless the Echo is overhead.
3. **The Observer.** A two-second glance. Skipping the Observer costs about 10 points of survival on Night 4.
4. **Corridors**, and only light one while the Wanderer is in it.
5. **Power.** Turn off what you do not need.

**Two at one door.** When the Patient and the Wanderer or Observer wait at the same door, shut the door *and* use the field. The door holds the other subject; the field sends the Patient home.

**Patient and Echo together** happens two or three times tonight. Shut the West door, stay silent and count the groans. The Echo usually leaves before the fifth push, and then you pull the field lever. If the fifth groan comes first, the door breaks. Hold your nerve: the Patient still needs a successful roll to come in, and the field becomes your first move the moment the vents go quiet.

### 5:00 to 6:00

Most losses again come in the last hour, when every subject is at its peak. If you still have the auxiliary cell, use it during a quiet moment early in this hour rather than waiting for 0%.

## Power

A careful player ends Night 4 with about **16%**, and about 1 run in 10 ends under 8%. A full power failure happens in about 4 runs in 100 with the habits, and in about 1 in 4 without them. The two habits that save the most power are **station lights low** and **field without the door**.

## What ends Night 4 runs

| Play style | Survival |
| --- | --- |
| Night 1 guide only | 56% |
| With the six habits | 87% |
| With the six habits, but slower (5 s reactions, Observer glance every 40 s) | 90% |

Slower is not worse here. Every subject waits several seconds at a doorway before its first try, so a calm reaction a few seconds later loses nothing and keeps doors open, and power in the battery, for longer.

With the habits, the Patient causes almost half of the remaining losses and the Echo about a third. Those are the moments when both were near the station together.
