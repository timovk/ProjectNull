# Night 5 guide

The last night. Every subject is out early and strong, and by 5 AM they are close to their maximum. The same scripted player that survives Night 1 every time survives Night 5 only about **3 runs in 4**, and only with every habit in this series. Expect to need a few attempts.

This guide assumes the [Night 1 guide](GUIDE_N1.md) and the six habits from the [Night 2 guide](GUIDE_N2.md). On Night 5, each of those habits matters.

Night 5 has its own terminal messages, and it ends differently. No spoilers here.

## The plan

### 12:00: set up in the first ten seconds

1. Station lights to **low**.
2. Glance at CAM 03 and CAM 06. The Patient leaves Isolation within minutes, by the back route through Cold Storage.
3. Know where the field lever, the fan switch, the radio and the door panels are without looking. You will use all of them under pressure tonight.

### 12:30 to 1:30: everything arrives

The Wanderer leaves at 12:30, the Echo at 12:54, the Observer at 1:00, and the Patient is usually already on its way. From now on:

- **Echo:** fan off, radio off, doors as they are, about 30 seconds of silence. It comes back about nine times tonight, sometimes only a minute after it left.
- **Patient:** field alone, door open, about 22 seconds. If the Echo is overhead: shut the West door, count the groans, field the moment the vents go quiet.
- **Wanderer and Observer:** shut the door they arrive at, within a few seconds. Light a corridor only while the Wanderer stands in it.
- **Observer:** a two-second glance every 20 to 30 seconds. Forgetting it costs more on Night 5 than on any other night.

### 2:00 to 5:00: protect the battery

You cannot afford waste tonight.

- Open each door as soon as its doorway is clear (unless the Echo is overhead).
- Never leave the field on after the Patient has withdrawn.
- Never leave a corridor light on after the Wanderer has gone.
- Leave the radio off for the whole night if you like; you lose its interference cue but gain a little power and quiet.

### 5:00 to 6:00: the last hour

Over half of all Night 5 losses happen here.

1. **Use the auxiliary cell before this hour if you can**, during a quiet moment: CAM 12 empty, no scraping. Its bang lets the Echo in if it is anywhere in the vents.
2. Expect the Patient at least once more. Keep enough power for about 25 seconds of field.
3. If the power fails, do not give up. Emergency lighting comes on, and you can brace a door by holding `E` on its yellow manual release. A braced door holds anything, the Patient included, but only while you keep holding, and only one door at a time. Bracing is noisy work, so the Echo can hear it, and every subject gets a little stronger in the dark. Brace the door that something is actually at, and remember that 6 AM may be closer than you think.

## Power

A careful player ends Night 5 with about **14%**, but about 1 run in 10 ends under 5%. A full power failure happens in about 7 runs in 100 with every habit, and in about 4 in 10 with only the Night 1 guide. Power is what decides most Night 5 runs.

## What ends Night 5 runs

| Play style | Survival |
| --- | --- |
| Night 1 guide only | 38% |
| Night 1 guide plus station lights low and corridor lights only when needed | 62% |
| With all six habits | 77% |
| With all six habits, but slower (5 s reactions, Observer glance every 40 s) | 75% |

With every habit, the Patient causes about half of the remaining losses and the Echo about a quarter. Most happen when the two of them are at the station together, or after a door has been forced. If you lose, it is worth asking: was the Echo overhead when the Patient arrived, and did I move a door while it was listening?
